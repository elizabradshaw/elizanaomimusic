My Studio

All of my knowledge is available to my students I teach cello, singing/songwriting, ukulele, guitar, and piano lessons. Many of my students end up studying several of these topics simultaneously.

I’m experienced with all ages But I currently focus on students ages 8 and older. I've been a dedicated teacher since 2022, and have taught over 200 group music classes and countless private lessons. I customize my lessons to the individual learning needs of each student.

I create a safe and inclusive environment My teaching is patient and gentle, with the focus on joy. I encourage students to explore their creativity, and focux on what is exciting for them. Music is a place to experiment, feel, and find yourself.
